Factory Reset¶

Performing a factory reset deletes all stored Wi-Fi credentials and configurations, restoring the device to its default factory state.

Manual Factory Reset (Hardware Button)¶

When the device cannot be reached over the network:

  1. Unplug the power supply from the device.
  2. Press and hold down the hardware button (on the Button Box, press the button at Row 1, Column 1).
  3. Plug the power supply back in while continuously holding down the button.
  4. Wait for more than 10 seconds (>10s).
  5. Release the button. The device will reset to factory defaults and restart in AP mode.

Software Factory Reset (Web Interface)¶

When the device is accessible on the local network:

  1. Open the device web interface in your browser: http://<DEVICE_IP>
  2. Navigate to the Factory reset menu option.
  3. Confirm the reset. The device will clear its settings and reboot.
